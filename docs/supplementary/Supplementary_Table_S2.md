# Supplementary Table S2: Model Parameters, Calibrations, and Biological Assumptions

This document compiles the exhaustive parameter definitions, mathematical symbols, biological interpretations, calibrated/reference values, status classifications, literature sources, and sensitivity analyses for the **BRCA1-ONSET** multi-scale modeling framework (*Palomero et al., 2026*).

In accordance with model verification and reporting standards, parameters are partitioned into:
- **Supplementary Table S2a**: Cell-scale agent-based model (500-cell terminal ductal lobular unit representation).
- **Supplementary Table S2b**: Tissue-scale population model ($10^7$-cell intact virtual ductal epithelium).

---

## Parameter Status Classification Scheme

Each parameter is assigned strictly to one of the following five standardized categories:
1. **`inferred`**: Quantitatively estimated from clinical penetrance benchmarks (Kuchenbaecker et al., 2017) via Bayesian Approximate Bayesian Computation with Sequential Monte Carlo (ABC-SMC, Generation 19, $N = 250$ particles; *Palomero et al., 2026*, Figure 3 and Figure 4a).
2. **`fixed structural assumption`**: Core architectural parameter defined *a priori* based on established biological literature, physiological constraints, or fundamental model formulation, and whose stability was corroborated via sensitivity analysis.
3. **`exploratory/reference setting`**: Reference or operating parameter setting identified during coarse/fine grid searches (e.g. exploratory pre-transformation sweeps in Exp 01) or theoretical perturbation sweeps (e.g. checkpoint bypass rates in Exp 09, homogeneous restraint in Exp 08).
4. **`derived`**: Composite parameter mathematically calculated from other parameters (e.g. $\delta_{\text{high}}/\delta_{\text{low}}$ ratio, pairwise ratios $R_1\dots R_6$, effective clonal doubling factor $2^{1/\tau_T}-1$).
5. **`tissue-scale calibrated`**: Parameter optimized in the intact $10^7$-cell virtual tissue via multi-objective fitting (jointly matching Kuchenbaecker et al. clinical penetrance, ~92.5% somatic BRCA1-null tumor composition, and empirical founder clonal complexity; *Palomero et al., 2026*, Figure 6).

## Cross-Scale Parameter Relationship & Scaling Principles

The two modeling scales represent complementary viewpoints of identical underlying biology:
1. **Target Population Scaling ($500 \to 10^7$ cells)**:
   - The cell-scale model simulates a single microscopic lobular unit ($N=500$ cells). At this scale, effective per-cell somatic transition rates ($\beta = 0.0456, \mu_{\text{TP53}} = 0.003$) reflect coarse-grained probabilities that at least one cell in the unit acquires the mutation.
   - The tissue-scale model explicitly simulates the complete anatomical at-risk epithelial ductal tree ($N_0 = 10^7$ cells). Because the pool of susceptible cells is $20,000\times$ larger, the calibrated mutation rates scale down ($\beta = 0.00275, \mu_{\text{TP53}} = 2.008 \times 10^{-5}$) to prevent premature synchronous multiclonal transformation and preserve clinical penetrance kinetics.
2. **Checkpoint & Barrier Rescaling ($\theta_{D1}, \theta_{D2}$)**:
   - In the discrete 500-cell ABM, damage counters advance in small integer/decimal increments with fixed thresholds $\theta_{D1}=2.0$ and $\theta_{D2}=5.0$.
   - In the continuous tissue-scale model, thresholds scale up to $\theta_{D1}=13.64$ and $\bar{\theta}_{D2}=31.79$, accompanied by inter-individual log-normal variation $\sigma_{\theta_{D2}} = 0.38$ that resolves the fundamental trade-off between rapid mid-life penetrance rise and the late-life non-penetrance plateau.
3. **Clonal Expansion Modeling ($\nu \to \tau_T$)**:
   - At the cell scale, neoplastic expansion is governed by the discrete division probability $\nu = 0.1569$ cycle$^{-1}$ under the "Big Bang" mode.
   - At the tissue scale, expansion is modeled as continuous exponential growth with volumetric doubling time $\tau_T = 0.65$ yr (~7.8 months), aligning with clinical triple-negative breast cancer growth kinetics.
4. **Invariant Core Constants**:
   - Resting turnover ($d = 0.001$ yr$^{-1}$), physiological background drift ($A_{\text{it}} = 10^{-5}$ yr$^{-1}$), HRD stress multiplier ($\kappa_{\text{BRCA1}} = 2.0$), and the diagnostic tumor detection threshold ($K_{\text{tum}} = 5\%$, representing 25/500 cells or $500,000/10^7$ cells) are conserved across both model tiers.
